## 1. State (The Only State That Exists)

\[
H_t \in \mathbb{R}^{k \times n}
\]

- \(n\): number of cortical neurons arranged on a 2-D sheet
- \(k\): internal vector state per neuron

Each neuron is **vector-valued**, not scalar.

There are:
- no layers
- no heads

Cortex is a **homogeneous recurrent sheet**.

## 2. Input (Diffuse Broadcast Injection)

External input enters cortex **without routing**:

\[
H_t \leftarrow H_t + E_t
\]

Where:
- \(E_t \in \mathbb{R}^{k \times n}\)
- typically smooth, low-rank, or spatially correlated

Properties:
- no addressing
- no selection
- no gating

Cortex does not choose where input goes.

## 3.2 Mesoscopic Transport (Topographic Convolutional Bridge)

To preserve spatial identity and enable high-fidelity mid-range communication, cortex includes a **fixed topographic transport operator**.

### Definition

Let:
\[
\mathcal{S}_{\text{conv}} : \mathbb{R}^{k \times n} \rightarrow \mathbb{R}^{k \times n}
\]

be a **depthwise separable convolution** applied independently to each feature channel.

\[
X^{(m)} = \mathcal{S}_{\text{conv}}(H_t)
\]

Kernel properties:
- compact spatial support
- distance-dependent weights
- translation-equivariant
- fixed topology
- no sparsity thresholds
- no explicit graph structure

### Multi-Scale Extension

To avoid a single privileged interaction distance, the operator may be expressed as a pyramid:

\[
X^{(m)} = \sum_{s} \mathcal{S}^{(s)}_{\text{conv}}(H_t)
\]

with kernels of increasing receptive field.

Interpretation:
- intracortical horizontal fibers
- mesoscopic signal diffusion
- preservation of high-dimensional structure

Small-world behavior emerges implicitly through repeated local transport.

\[

\]

Interpretation:
- NE amplifies global coordination

No explicit error signals are used.

## 3.5 Local Lateral Inhibition (Competition Without Global Statistics)

Cortex performs **local**, not global, normalization.

Let neurons be indexed by position \(i\).

Let \(K_{ij}\) be a fixed local inhibitory kernel.

### Subtractive inhibition
\[
\mu_i =
\sum_{j \in \mathcal{N}(i)} K_{ij} \, H_{\text{raw},j}
\]

### Divisive inhibition
\[
\gamma_i =
\sum_{j \in \mathcal{N}(i)} K_{ij} \, |H_{\text{raw},j}|
\]

### Chemically Modulated Inhibition Gain

Let:
- \(\beta_i = \beta_0 \cdot h_{\text{ACh}}(\text{ACh}_{t,i})\)

High ACh sharpens inhibition; low ACh relaxes it.

### State Update
\[
H_{t+1,i} =
\frac{H_{\text{raw},i} - \alpha \mu_i}
{\epsilon + \beta_i \gamma_i}
\]

Effects:
- local competition
- sparse activation
- contrast enhancement
- scale stabilization
