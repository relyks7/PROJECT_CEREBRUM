It makes **no reference** to:
- gradients

- objectives
- backpropagation

This object is the **irreducible cortical atom**.

## 1. State (The Only State That Exists)

\[
H_t \in \mathbb{R}^{k \times n}
\]

- \(n\): number of cortical neurons arranged on a 2-D sheet  
- \(k\): internal vector state per neuron  

Each neuron is **vector-valued**, not scalar.

There are:
- no layers
- no heads
- no modules
- no explicit hierarchy

Cortex is a **homogeneous recurrent sheet**.

## 2. Input (Diffuse Broadcast Injection)

External input enters cortex **without routing**:

\[
H_t \leftarrow H_t + E_t
\]

Where:
- \(E_t \in \mathbb{R}^{k \times n}\)
- typically smooth, low-rank, or spatially correlated

Properties:
- no addressing
- no selection
- no gating

Cortex does not choose where input goes.  
Competition determines what persists.

## 3.5 Local Lateral Inhibition (Corrected)

### Core Principle

Cortex **does not** use dense inhibitory matrices.

Inhibition is:
- **local**
- **fixed-topology**
- **feature-reduced**
- **spatially structured**

No \(n \times n\) inhibitory matrices exist.

---

### 3.5.1 Inhibitory Neighborhood

Each neuron \(i\) has a local neighborhood:

\[
\mathcal{N}(i) = \{ j \mid \|x_i - x_j\| \le r \}
\]

Kernels are:
- compact
- distance-weighted
- translation-equivariant

---

### 3.5.2 Subtractive Inhibition (Local Mean Suppression)

Removes **shared local excitation**:

\[
\mu_i
=
\sum_{j \in \mathcal{N}(i)}
K^{(\mu)}_{ij}
\;
\langle H_{\text{raw},j} \rangle_k
\]

Where:
- \(K^{(\mu)}\): fixed local kernel (e.g. radius-3)
- \(\langle \cdot \rangle_k\): reduction over feature dimension
- \(\mu \in \mathbb{R}^n\)

Interpretation:
- removes common-mode activity
- enforces contrast
- stabilizes population activity

---

### 3.5.3 Divisive Inhibition (Local Gain Control)

Normalizes **local activity magnitude**:

\[
\gamma_i
=
\sum_{j \in \mathcal{N}(i)}
K^{(\gamma)}_{ij}
\;
\langle |H_{\text{raw},j}| \rangle_k
\]

Where:
- \(K^{(\gamma)}\): fixed local kernel (e.g. radius-7)
- \(\gamma \in \mathbb{R}^n\)

Interpretation:
- scale stabilization
- prevents local blow-up
- supports sparse competition

---

### 3.5.4 Chemically Modulated Inhibitory Gain

High ACh → sharper competition
